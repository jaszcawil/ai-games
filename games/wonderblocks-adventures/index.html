How to Play

Goal: guide all three friends to their own matching goal tile in every room.

🟢 Go is fast and fearless — only Go can safely cross pits.

🔴 Stop is strong — push crates onto plates to open doors, or push a crate into a pit to bridge it.

🟡 Again is clever — step on levers to open doors for everyone.

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to move. Press 1, 2, 3 (or click a portrait) to switch character. R to restart the level.
