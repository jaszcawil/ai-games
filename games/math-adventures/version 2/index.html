How To Play

Move: Use the joystick (or WASD / arrow keys / gamepad stick) to walk.

Jump: Tap the JUMP button (or Space / gamepad A) to hop over gaps.

Special Move: Tap the sparkle button to use your hero's special power!

Math Time: Answer questions correctly to open the path forward. It's okay to get one wrong — just try again!

Save: Your game saves automatically. You can also save to a file on your computer any time from the Pause menu.
